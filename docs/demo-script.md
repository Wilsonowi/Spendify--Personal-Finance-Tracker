# Two-Minute Demonstration

1. Sign in to a local synthetic account and show the empty monthly overview.
2. Open Imports and upload all three files from `fixtures/`.
3. Explain that originals are encrypted before storage and OCR runs privately.
4. Review the RM12.50 `X001 DAVIDS CHICK` purchase, rename it to `David's Chick`,
   choose `Food & Dining`, and save the private merchant rule.
5. Review and confirm the Public Bank RM100 debit and TNG RM100 credit.
6. Open the transfer suggestion and point out amount, direction, account pair,
   timestamp, and related-reference evidence.
7. Confirm the pair and show that RM100 moves to internal transfer volume rather
   than monthly spending.
8. Show monthly spending at RM12.50 and add a food budget.
9. Export CSV, explain spreadsheet-formula protection, and demonstrate exact
   upload duplicate rejection.
10. Finish on Privacy Controls and delete one encrypted original explicitly.

## Statement Variant

1. Open Imports, choose **Statement**, and upload the two PDFs in
   `fixtures/statements/` for calendar year 2026.
2. Let the automatic redirect open January. Point out net spending, provider
   coverage, the partial-period label, and the separate RM100 transfer volume.
3. Show that the known merchant keyword was categorized automatically while the
   unknown purchase posted under provisional `Uncategorized`; point out the
   category-quality indicator rather than a blocking review queue.
4. Show RM13.50 of January purchases. Explain that the unique RM100 bank-to-TNG
   pair reconciled automatically and remains outside spending.
5. Change the month selector, then export the ledger with the 2026 date range
   and show its provider type, resolution method, and treatment columns.
